# 🎬 My Favorite K-Dramas


## 🌅 1. **Mr. Sunshine**
> *"Love is like the moon — even if it waxes and wanes, it’s always there."*  
A historical masterpiece set in the late Joseon era, *Mr. Sunshine* beautifully portrays courage, loyalty, and forbidden love in times of war.

---

## 🏡 2. **Reply 1988**
> *"Youth may fade, but the memories remain forever."*  
A warm, nostalgic drama about friendship, family, and growing up in the late 80s. Every episode feels like a hug from the past.

---

## 🍊 3. **When Life Gives You Tangerines**
> *"Even small moments can be sweet — like a tangerine in winter."*  
A heartfelt slice-of-life series about finding joy and healing in everyday life. Gentle, poetic, and emotionally rich.
